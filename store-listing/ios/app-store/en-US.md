## Description

Cubegin is a simple speedcubing timer.

- View scramble notation and diagrams, and use WCA inspection with voice prompts.
- Practice with targeted scrambles and check solution hints when you need them.
- Organize your solves into groups and track your progress with averages and trend charts.
- Sync solves, groups, and key preferences across devices with iCloud.
