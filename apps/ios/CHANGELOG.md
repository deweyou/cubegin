# Cubegin for iOS changelog

[简体中文](CHANGELOG_ZH.md)

## 1.0.0

- Time solves with touch or a hardware keyboard, with optional WCA inspection and customizable swipe shortcuts.
- Review rolling averages and trends, organize solves into groups, and import results from DCTimer.
- Sync solve times, groups, and supported settings across devices signed into the same iCloud account.
- In Settings → About, read the changelog and check for updates on the App Store.
