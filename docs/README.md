Cubegin is a speedcubing timer and training project. It includes a native iPhone and iPad app and a reusable Rust cube engine.

## iPhone and iPad app

Time solves, practice with event and training scrambles, and track results by group. View rolling averages and charts, or bring existing results over from DCTimer. The app uses SwiftUI and supports iOS and iPadOS 26 or later.

[Explore the iOS app](https://github.com/deweyou/cubegin/blob/main/apps/ios/README.md)

## Cube engine

The Rust engine provides standard and training scrambles, full and auxiliary solving, SVG puzzle diagrams, and 3D move playback. It is available through Swift, Kotlin and Web/WASM.
